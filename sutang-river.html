01 • River information

A verified river profile

The strongest baseline used here is the Bangladesh Government's Classification of Wetlands of Bangladesh — Annexure 1: Rivers of Bangladesh. It identifies Sutang as BWDB Id NE-82.

Government record

Origin & course

The government dataset records the offtake/source area as India. It describes the river's travel through Chunarughat, Habiganj Sadar and Lakhai upazilas of Habiganj District.

Government record

Outfall & form

The same dataset records the outfall as the Meghna (Upper) River, describes Sutang as meandering, and gives a flood-flow slope of about 1 cm/km.

02 • Then

History & people

The project will preserve the river as a historical record, not only as a pollution story.

Earlier role

Recent reporting describes Sutang as an important communication route in the haor region when it carried water throughout the year. This is retained as a reported historical account, not as a government measurement.

Industrialisation & pollution

Research and reporting document the connection between industrial effluent channels around the Alipur industrial area and the Sutang system, including the Shoiljura canal.

Legal / institutional response

In 2024, the National River Conservation Commission published a notice concerning a committee formed under High Court directions to monitor ETP operation and pollution-control measures for Sutang and the connected Shoiljura canal.

2026 restoration work

Bangladesh's e-GP system records a BWDB tender under the project “Revitalization and Pollution-free of Sutang River in Habiganj District” for dredging/re-excavation of a 6.5 km section in Lakhai.

Archive rule: old photographs, newspaper scans and historical maps will be added only with a traceable archive/source credit. Copyrighted material should be linked or displayed with appropriate permission rather than copied into the project.
03 • Now

Present condition

The current picture combines government monitoring infrastructure, academic research and documented local impacts. Where the evidence is not yet complete, the project says so.

Documented problem

Industrial & water pollution

Academic work links industrial effluent from the Alipur industrial area to the Sutang through the Shoiljura effluent-carrying canal. The National River Conservation Commission has also documented institutional action specifically addressing pollution of Sutang and Shoiljura.

Recent research

Microplastics

Habiganj Agricultural University reported research detecting 6.67–46.6 microplastic particles per litre in Sutang water. The same report says 51 plastic particles were identified from the digestive tracts of 30 sampled fish.

Government monitoring

Water-quality station

BWDB's surface-water-quality database lists SW280 — Sutang Railway Bridge in Habiganj Sadar, with records available from July 2022 through August 2024 in the published station summary.

Data gap

Old vs current photographs

A proper before/after gallery is still a research task. We should collect dated images from government archives, newspapers and permission-based local sources and attach the original date/location to every image.

04 • Why

Causes & consequences

Industrial effluent

Documented as a major pressure around the industrial area and Shoiljura connection.

Waste & plastics

Microplastic research provides direct evidence of plastic contamination in river water and sampled fish.

Siltation / reduced channel capacity

Recent reporting connects the river's altered condition with inadequate dredging; government restoration procurement now includes re-excavation.

Encroachment & unplanned development

These are documented concerns in the wider Habiganj river system and should be mapped separately with primary evidence for each location.

05 • Government action

What authorities are doing

2024 • NRCC

Pollution-control monitoring

NRCC published a notice for a committee meeting under High Court directions concerning ETP operation, modern pollution-control equipment and monitoring for Sutang and Shoiljura.

2026 • BWDB / e-GP

Re-excavation / dredging

An official e-GP tender records a 6.5 km dredging/re-excavation package from km 6.500 to km 13.000 in Lakhai under the Sutang revitalisation and pollution-free project. The tender record shows a tentative completion date of 15 August 2027.

06 • Future

How the river can be protected

These are project recommendations, not claims that every measure is already approved or implemented.

1. Stop pollution at the source

Require effective ETP operation, continuous compliance monitoring and transparent enforcement for industrial discharges.

2. Restore the channel scientifically

Use surveyed cross-sections, sediment studies and hydrological evidence to determine where re-excavation is appropriate.

3. Protect connected canals

Treat Shoiljura and other connected waterways as part of the river system rather than solving only the main channel.

4. Build a public monitoring record

Publish dated water-quality measurements, photographs, pollution incidents, government actions and project progress in one transparent timeline.

5. Protect fisheries & communities

Track fish biodiversity, livelihood effects and safe water use with local institutions and researchers.

6. Preserve the history

Digitise verified old maps, photographs, oral-history interviews and newspaper archives with source/date/location metadata.
