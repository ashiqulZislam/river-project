Shonai River

The Shonai River is a vital waterway in Bangladesh, flowing through the capital city of Dhaka. This research project aims to provide a comprehensive profile of the river, its history, current condition, and ongoing restoration efforts.

Learn More

Overview

The Shonai River has played a significant role in the cultural and economic development of Dhaka. However, it has faced severe pollution and encroachment issues over the years. This project seeks to document the river's current state and explore potential restoration strategies.

History

Historically, the Shonai River was a bustling waterway that supported trade and transportation. Over time, urbanization and industrialization have led to its degradation. Understanding its historical significance is crucial for effective restoration efforts.

Current Condition

The river is currently facing challenges such as water pollution, illegal dumping, and loss of biodiversity. This section provides an in-depth analysis of the river's present condition based on recent studies and observations.

Restoration Status

Various initiatives have been undertaken to restore the Shonai River, including community clean-up drives, policy interventions, and awareness campaigns. This section highlights the progress made and the challenges that remain.
