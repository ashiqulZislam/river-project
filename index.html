TRANSPARENCY

Sources matter.

This project should distinguish verified facts from community memories or observations. Government agencies, research papers, historical maps, satellite imagery, and properly attributed photographs can be listed here as the project grows.

Source policy

Every river profile should include a “Sources” section. Do not present an estimate or unverified claim as an official fact.
